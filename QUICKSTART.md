# Moshpit quick start

Open IN, import a clip you own, and mark a range. Add it to the timeline in MOSH.
Play or step beneath the preview. Select a block to adjust its effects; GRAPH
shows its processing connections. Undo restores the previous edit.

Save your editable project as `.mosh`. In OUT, choose an output folder and render.
Projects reference footage: keep that footage available when reopening or sharing.
No sample footage or personal projects are bundled in this source preparation.
