SOURCE ASSETS AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

Project SVG artwork is shipped as source, under the project's licence where
owned by the project author. App and hardware names do not imply endorsement.
No personal footage, sample projects or bundled font binaries are in this
source export. No marketplace icon pack is included.

Moshpit obtains Inter and JetBrains Mono through its locked Fontsource packages.
Their SIL Open Font Licence texts and copyright notices are in third-party/;
they are not relicensed under MIT. Runtime/FFmpeg/dependency binary distribution
needs a separate exact-package review before downloads are offered.

CT icon source provenance is recorded in the existing stroke renderer and
procedural app-icon generator. The local audit retains the original provenance
notes privately; private folder paths are not part of this document.
